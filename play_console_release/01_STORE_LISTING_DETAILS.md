### 2. Full Store Description (Max 4,000 characters)

```text
Turn your smartphone into the ultimate Universal TV Remote Control! Whether you have an older LED/CRT TV requiring an Infrared (IR) blaster or a modern Smart TV running Android TV, Google TV, Samsung Tizen, LG webOS, or Sony Bravia, this app provides seamless, zero-lag control right from your pocket.

Designed with a clean, clutter-free minimalist interface, our dynamic remote adjusts its buttons automatically depending on whether your TV is an Indian household favorite (Vu, Mi, Thomson, Micromax, Lloyd, Onida, BPL, Sanyo) or a global titan (Samsung, LG, Sony, Panasonic, TCL, Hisense, Philips).

=========================================
🔥 3-IN-1 HYBRID CONNECTIVITY PROTOCOLS
=========================================
1. INFRARED (IR BLASTER):
   • Works on Android phones with built-in hardware IR emitter (Xiaomi/Mi/Redmi, Poco, Huawei, Honor, Vivo, Oppo, HTC, LeEco, Techno).
   • Controls traditional non-smart TVs, Set-Top Boxes, DTH (Tata Play, Airtel DTH, Dish TV, Sun Direct, DD Free Dish), and DVD players.
   • Supports 38kHz NEC, RC5, RC6, and Sony SIRC protocols with Pronto HEX compatibility.

2. BLUETOOTH LOW ENERGY (BLE):
   • Connect to modern Bluetooth-enabled Smart TVs with fast pairing.
   • Ideal for navigation, D-pad, media controls, and voice-assisted TV units.

3. SMART WI-FI (LAN & UPnP/SSDP):
   • Automatic discovery of Smart TVs connected to your local home Wi-Fi network.
   • Full compatibility with:
     - Samsung Smart TVs (Tizen OS WebSocket protocol)
     - LG Smart TVs (webOS secure pairing)
     - Sony Bravia (IRCC REST / IP Control)
     - Android TV & Google TV (Chromecast & remote protocol)
     - Roku TV, Fire TV, and PatchWall OS.

=========================================
📺 EXTENSIVE BRAND DATABASE SUPPORT
=========================================
• Indian Market Leaders: MarQ by Flipkart, Onida (Classic & Fire TV), Vu, Mi (Xiaomi), OnePlus TV, Realme, Kodak, Thomson, Croma, Micromax, Lloyd, BPL, Videocon, Intex, Sansui, Daiwa / Shinco, Sanyo, T-Series, Weston.
• Global Flagships: Samsung, LG, Sony Bravia, Hisense, Philips, Panasonic, TCL, Haier, Toshiba, Sharp Aquos, Skyworth, Insignia, Vizio.
• DTH & Set-Top Boxes: Tata Play, Airtel Digital TV, Videocon D2H, Dish TV, Hathway, Den Networks, Siti Cable, GTPL.

=========================================
⭐ KEY USER-CENTRIC FEATURES
=========================================
✔ Dynamic UI Adaptation: Remote layout shifts between Smart OTT Mode (Netflix, YouTube, Prime Video, Disney+ Hotstar shortcuts, Touch D-pad) and Classic TV Mode (Numeric 0-9 keys, Source/AV, Picture, Aspect).
✔ Haptic Vibration Feedback: Tactile sensation with every keypress so you never have to look down at the screen.
✔ Multi-Room TV Profiles: Save your Living Room TV, Bedroom TV, and Office TV with one-tap switching.
✔ Offline Remote Cache: Once synced via our cloud database, your IR codes work 100% offline without internet.
✔ Fast Code Auto-Tuning: If your exact model is unknown, our 3-step Quick Code Tester cycles power pulses to find your TV's matching signal instantly.

=========================================
⚠️ DEVICE COMPATIBILITY NOTICE
=========================================
• For IR Mode: Your Android smartphone must feature a built-in Infrared (IR) hardware transmitter.
• For Wi-Fi Mode: Ensure both your phone and Smart TV are connected to the same 2.4GHz / 5GHz Wi-Fi router.
• Phones lacking an IR blaster can still control all Smart TVs using Bluetooth and Wi-Fi!

Download now and never search between sofa cushions for a lost TV remote again!
```
